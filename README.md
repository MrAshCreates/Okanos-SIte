# Okanos - Navigate the Sea of Your Finances

**Okanos** (oh-KAH-nohss) - From the Greek myth of **Okeanos**, the primordial ocean and boundary of the known world, merged with the Japanese **お金 (okane)** meaning "money."

Your app becomes the map of someone's money-world, teaching you to stay ahead of the currents and avoid the tides of finance.

---

## 🌊 The Ocean Metaphor

The financial world flows like water. Okanos uses ocean metaphors to make finance intuitive and memorable:

- **Tides** = income cycles
- **Currents** = spending patterns  
- **Depth** = credit history
- **Storms** = debt, emergencies
- **Compass** = budgeting plans
- **Treasures** = savings, investing rewards
- **Voyages** = financial goals and quests
- **Ocean Health** = financial health score
- **Drift Warnings** = overspending alerts

## 🧭 Features

### Okanos Beta (formerly Financial Command Center)

Navigate your complete financial picture:

- **Dashboard** - Chart your financial course
- **Budget** - Manage your financial tides  
- **Credit Cards** - Navigate credit currents
- **Loans** - Stay afloat with debt
- **Income** - Track incoming tides
- **Reports** - Deep dive into your ocean

### Dynamic UX Modes

- **Simple Mode** - For beginners, shows only essentials
- **Normal Mode** - Balanced experience for most users
- **Advanced Mode** - Dense layouts and power features for financial professionals

### Financial Strategies

Five distinct budgeting strategies that automatically adjust allocations:

1. **50/30/20 Rule** - Balanced approach
2. **All Cash** - Debt-averse strategy
3. **Only Credit** - Rewards maximizer
4. **Investing Focused** - Wealth building
5. **Buy, Borrow, Die** - Leverage strategy
